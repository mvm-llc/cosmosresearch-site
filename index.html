THE CONSTELLATION BUILD

Over 12 weeks, we map the patterns driving your product decisions and build a system that applies the same rigor to product decisions as you do to financial capital. Your team makes decisions that justify every dollar invested and fully owns the system.

What you're experiencing

  • Product capital is being allocated without defensible ROI projections. When you're asked to justify product spend—whether by the board, investors, or your finance team—you don't have the data to back it up.
  • Features get prioritized based on stakeholder pressure, competitive reaction, or assumptions. Budget is allocated without proof of return. You're building constantly, but ROI isn't clear.
  • Without a system to track ROI from decision to delivery, you can't connect product investments to revenue growth.

What you'll receive

  • Pattern Map showing where capital is being wasted and what's driving your product decisions.
  • Prioritization Framework (Cost Per Customer system) so you know what's worth building.
  • Evaluation Protocols (30/60/90 tracking) to prove returns post-launch.
  • Complete roadmap with financial projections for every priority—defensible at the board level.
  • Team training so your team owns the system going forward.

FAQ

How does the 12-Week Constellation Build work?
We map the patterns driving your product decisions through data analysis, team conversations, and customer feedback. Then we build a system that applies financial rigor to every product investment using Cost Per Customer Impacted. By week 12, your team makes investment decisions that justify the capital and owns the system independently.
Who is this for?

This is for CPOs and VPs of Product at PE-backed SaaS companies ($30M-$50M ARR), and Operating Partners at PE/VC firms deploying advisors into portfolio companies.

The system applies financial rigor to product capital allocation, makes every investment decision defensible with data, and connects product decisions directly to revenue growth.

What makes your system different?

Most product teams have data but no system to turn it into defensible capital allocation decisions.

The system I build does three things:

Makes every decision measurable. Cost Per Customer Impacted (Cost to build ÷ customers impacted) becomes the baseline for every product investment.

Reveals what's driving decisions. Pattern mapping shows organizational, team, and product patterns so you address root causes instead of symptoms.

Tracks returns post-launch. Evaluation protocols at 30/60/90 days prove which investments delivered and which didn't.

Your team learns to run the system independently—applying the same financial rigor to product capital as you do everywhere else.

What do I need to have in place before we start?
Access to your product data, support tickets, and key team members for conversations. You don't need a huge team or perfect documentation. I work with what's already there and help you see what matters.
